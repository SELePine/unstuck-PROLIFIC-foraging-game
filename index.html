How to play

In the game, you (red square) and your partner (blue square) will collect berries from bushes.

Use WASD to move. Purple berries earn points; orange berries lose points.

You'll play a 3-minute round and then continue to a short survey.

First, practice moving and collecting a berry. Tutorial points won't count toward your game score.
